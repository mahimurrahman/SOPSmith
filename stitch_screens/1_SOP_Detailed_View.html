Omnichannel Content Governance & Workflow

Marketing Ops Social Strategy High Priority

Strategic Objective

To standardize the end-to-end lifecycle of social media assets—from ideation through multi-stakeholder approval to automated distribution—ensuring rigorous brand compliance and data-driven engagement optimization.

Operational Scope

Encompasses all global social ecosystems including LinkedIn (Executive Thought Leadership), Instagram (Visual Storytelling), and X/Twitter (Real-time Engagement). This protocol governs both organic initiatives and paid amplification performance assets.

Execution Protocol

01

Creative Development

Synthesize quarterly campaign themes into high-fidelity copy drafts and visual concepts. Ensure all assets align with the centralized design system tokens.

02

Technical Peer Audit

Submit work for cross-functional review. Focus on accessibility standards, localization requirements, and platform-specific formatting nuances.

03

Stakeholder Finalization

Promote assets to 'Awaiting Final Approval' status. Regional Directors must provide sign-off by Thursday 17:00 EST for subsequent week scheduling.

04

Automated Deployment

Execute scheduling via Sprout Social using the 'Optimal Send Time' algorithm based on 90-day historical engagement heatmaps.
